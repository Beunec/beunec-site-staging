### Key Technical Differentiators

* **Claude Cowork's "Multiplayer" Harness:** Unlike traditional solo agents, Anthropic has positioned Cowork for asynchronous, team-wide delegation. The agent exists in shared channels, allowing multiple users to observe, correct, and collaborate with it securely.
* **Perplexity Computer's "GUI Control" Advantage:** Instead of relying purely on APIs, Perplexity Computer acts as a general-purpose digital worker. It natively operates graphical interfaces clicking, scrolling, and typing across disconnected local desktop and web applications.
* **Microsoft's "Work IQ & Semantic Index":** This platform "knows" your company organizational chart. It doesn't just know what a document is; it understands who wrote it, who they report to, and why it matters to your current meeting based on social and network signals.

### Recommendations Based on Operational Needs

* **Avoid Claude Cowork** if you need a standalone desktop automation bot to click through legacy local software. It excels in secure, integrated API workspaces and collaborative team chats.
* **Avoid Gemini / Copilot** if your work involves unfiltered competitor analysis or grey-area research, as their enterprise safety filters and corporate bias sanitize results heavily compared to dedicated search models.
* **Avoid Perplexity Computer** if you want an AI embedded seamlessly inside a multiplayer team space (like a shared Slack or Teams channel). It is designed to act as a highly capable, cross-app solo desktop assistant.
