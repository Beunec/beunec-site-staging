# Observability

Observability is the Aselius app that shows what every engine turn in your workspace actually did: success or failure, retries, tokens, cost and latency. Teams can audit cost and reliability without anyone reading private conversations.

## At a glance

- **What it is:** the measurement app of Aselius, live today beside Workspace, Automation and Manage Teams.
- **You get:** per-turn and roll-up metrics for your own operations.
- **Never shown or stored:** conversation content.
- **Best for:** auditing spend, reliability and retries across a team.
- **Where:** open **Observability** from the platform rail at [aselius.beunec.com](https://aselius.beunec.com).

## What you see

- **Outcomes:** success, failure and retry counts, per turn and rolled up.
- **Usage:** tokens and cost for each turn.
- **Speed:** latency per turn.
- **Artificial Experience activity:** the kinds of memory events that happened, so it is not a black box.
- **Tools and services:** activity from tools and services where it applies to your workspace.

## What you do not see

- **Conversation content.** None is recorded in Observability, by design.
- **Which models engines use.** Beunec does not disclose the models behind its engines.

## Why it matters

Governed AI work needs an honest record. When a turn succeeds only on a retry, spends credits, or uses Artificial Experience, Observability makes it visible. That lets a team check cost and reliability without reading anyone's prompts.
